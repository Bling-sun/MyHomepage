THE BACKGROUND

A little about me.

Hi, I'm Bing Sun, a master's student in the IIAU Lab at Dalian University of Technology, with graduation expected in June 2027.

My research focuses on autonomous robot navigation, motion planning, and task understanding and action generation with vision–language–action (VLA) models. I work on connecting perception, planning, and learned control policies for real robots.

During my internship at RoboScience, I worked on semantic mapping and navigation, robotic parcel sorting, and long-horizon tea preparation. I also participated in multi-agent research through a Tsinghua University exchange and an NSFC major project.

LECO

Learning Direct Control from Optimization for Dynamic Target Tracking

ICRA 2027Under review
Bing Sun · First author

Across tested obstacle densities, linear-velocity jerk RMS decreased by 33.2%–47.6% relative to the next-best baseline; computation took 4.25–5.63 ms per cycle, with success rates above 80%. Validated on HUNTER 2.0 and Unitree G1-D platforms.

Method & details+

Transfers trajectory optimization objectives into differentiable losses and backpropagates through kinematic integration to learn a control policy. The method combines target visibility, obstacle clearance, and control smoothness with decaying expert guidance, a predictive safety lock, and a classical planning fallback.

UCON

UCON: Uncertainty-aware Navigation with Historical Re-association in Dynamic Environments

IROS 2026Accepted
Bing Sun, Yue Lin, Yongsheng Yuan, Yang Liu, Dong Wang, Huchuan Lu

Evaluated in complex dynamic environments through simulation and experiments on an omnidirectional mobile robot.

Method & details+

Uses point-level reassociation with historical point-cloud segments and Kalman filtering to maintain dynamic-object tracking through occlusions, missed detections, and identity switches. Predicted states and covariances form uncertainty sectors that translate dynamic collision risk into differentiable trajectory costs.
